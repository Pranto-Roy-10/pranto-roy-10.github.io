🧬 THESIS ★AUG 2026 · RESEARCH
// FINAL QUEST — THE GENOME TRIALS · GRADED A+

TriLens-MoE-Stack — Undergraduate Thesis

PYTORCH · MIXTURE-OF-EXPERTS · MULTI-VIEW LEARNING · XGBOOST
trilens — mean PR-AUC across 7 antibiotics
  • Lineage-aware multi-drug antibiotic resistance prediction in E. coli — 6,962 genomes read through three representations, a separate neural lens on each, gated by a mixture-of-experts and stacked with classical learners
  • 0.849 mean PR-AUC across seven antibiotics under a random split, against 0.803 for the gradient-boosting baseline
  • Held whole sequence types out and the score fell to 0.634 — 2,000 resampling rounds separated prevalence shift from a genuine 0.126 generalisation gap
  • Recovered gyrA and parC as the leading ciprofloxacin markers, and reported honestly that attention weights were not a reliable explanation
  • Surveyed fourteen prior studies: only two split on population structure, none corrected for prevalence shift — the basis for proposing an evaluation protocol

Supervisor: Dr. Md. Golam Rabiul Alam · Co-supervisors: Dr. Swakkhar Shatabda, Md. Saiful Bari Siddiqui · Accepted 10 August 2026
